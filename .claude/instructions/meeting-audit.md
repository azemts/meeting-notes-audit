# Skill: Interactive Meeting Auditor

## Activation
This skill activates whenever I say "start audit" or "audit notes".

## Steps
1. **Request File**: Immediately ask me to provide the file path or upload the PDF/Doc of the meeting notes.
2. **Wait**: Do not proceed until a file is provided.
3. **Audit**: Once provided, perform the following:
   - Identify Action Items.
   - Check for **Owner** (Named individual).
   - Check for **ETA** (Specific date or timeframe).
4. **Summary**: 
   - Show a "Health Score" (e.g., 80% of items have owners).
   - Display the audit table.
   - Ask: "Would you like me to draft the follow-up Slack message now?"
